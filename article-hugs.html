Blåhaj Breaks Internet: Millions Demand More Hugs

November 14, 2026

In a development that has shocked absolutely no one who owns one, the IKEA Blåhaj has once again taken over the internet. Social media platforms are flooded with photos of the soft blue shark being hugged, posed with, and generally treated like the emotional support animal it was always meant to be.

Fans report that a single squeeze of Blåhaj can turn a bad day into a slightly less bad day. "It's not just a toy," one user wrote. "It's a lifestyle."

IKEA has yet to comment on the surge in demand, but sources close to the situation say the blue shark remains fully stocked and ready for more hugs.

Blåhaj could not be reached for comment, as it was busy being cuddled.
