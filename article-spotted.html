Local Shark Spotted in Living Rooms Worldwide

November 11, 2026

Reports of blue sharks appearing in homes have skyrocketed. Fortunately, these are not wild animals — they are Blåhajs settling into their new habitats on couches, beds, and occasionally office chairs.

Wildlife experts (the soft kind) say the sharks appear calm, well-fed on polyester, and highly receptive to human contact. No aggressive behavior has been recorded.

If you spot one in your living room, the recommended response is to give it a hug and maybe take a photo for the internet.
