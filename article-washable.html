IKEA Confirms: Blåhaj is Machine Washable (and Still Cuddly After)

November 12, 2026

In news that will comfort parents and owners of slightly sticky Blåhajs everywhere, IKEA has confirmed that the beloved shark is machine washable. Warm water, durable-press cycle, tumble dry low — and it comes out just as soft as before.

A recent informal study conducted by several internet users found that huggability remains at 100% post-wash. "I was worried it would get stiff," said one tester. "But no. Still the perfect pillow."

Official care instructions remain: do not bleach, do not iron, do not dry clean. Just wash, dry, and resume hugging.
